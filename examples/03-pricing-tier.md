# Example 03 · "How should we price our B2B AI product?"

> When the founder is staring at a blank pricing page.

---

## The decision

**Founder asks:**

> "We're an AI GTM tool. Our users are AI startup founders (mostly Series A-B, 5-50 person companies). They currently waste ~5 hours/week manually finding creators on X. We save them that time. What should we charge?"

---

## Compass output

```markdown

**Value layer (梁宁 #36):** Currently functional value only

- Asset: NOT YET unlocked — no accumulating user data/asset

**→ Pricing today should target functional + emerging emotional ($99-$299 range)**

- Avoid anchoring against competitors (you don't have direct ones yet)
```

```
## Why this works

**Identifies the value layer ceiling:**
- 梁宁 frame shows you're at functional-only — this caps you at ~$299
- To unlock $999+ tiers, you need to add emotional + asset value layers
- This is a roadmap signal, not just a pricing answer

**Uses anchoring like a pro:**
- "$299 = 2.7% of a BDR" is a killer line
- Skylar's金融 background applied: it's a unit economics comparison, not a feature pitch

**Pre-empts the free-forever trap:**
- 大败局 II warned against 庞氏化 (build for paying customer Day 1)
- Recommendation explicitly avoids "free forever" trap

**Names the next bottleneck:**
- Hits $999 ceiling without emotional + asset value layers
- Tells founder where to invest product effort to lift pricing later
```
